Introduction

Welcome to our website where we are giving comprehensive information about our solar sytem. Our Solar System is found in the Milky Ways Spiral Galaxy in the universe. -The Solar Sytem is a collection of celestial bodies-the sun, planets, moons, asteroids, meteoroids, and dwarf planets that are held by the sun's gravitationational force as they orbit around it.



-It was formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a large cloud of gas and dust called the solar nebula-it based in the Nebula Hypothesis which best explains the formation of the solar system, others are like the passing star theory and the big bang theory. -The Nebula Hypothesis continues to state that solar nebula cloud collapsed under gravity, most materials gathered at the center to form the sun, while the rest flattened into a spinning disk that became the planets and other heavenly bodies.



The Sun
-Is the star at the center of our solar system.It is massive ball of hot, glowing gas, primarily composed of Hydrogen amd Helium. The sun's surface temperature is about 5500 Degrees Celcius. It is so massive that it makes up about 99.8% of the mass in our solar system. It provides light, heat, and energy for all the planets.


The Planets
-Are celestial bodies that are relatively large enough for their force of gravity to make them round or nearly to a round shape, in which they orbit in a clear path elliptical orbit around the sun. Types of Planets
  1. Terrestrial Planets-Rocky with solid surfaces such as Earth, Mars, Mercury and Venus.
  2. Gas Giants(Jovians)-Large gaseous planets such as Jupiter and Saturn.
  3. Ice Giants-Composed mostly of water, ammonia, and methane ices such as Uranus, and Neptune.
  4. Dwarf Planets-Are celestial bodies that are typically smaller than planets and reside in the outer reaches of the solar system, they typically do not have a clear orbit as they go round the sun since some objects cross their orbit in which some are larger than them.
They Include:
  1. Pluto - Located in the Kuiper Belt, a region of icy bodies beyond Neptune.
  2. Eris - Also in the Kuiper Belt, slighty larger than Pluto.
  3. Ceres - The largest object in the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter.
  4. Haumea - An irregularly shaped dwarf planet in the Kuiper Belt.
  5. Makemake - Another Kuiper Belt object with a highly reflective surface.




Mercury


-Is the closest planet to the sun and the smallest planet in our solar system. Its distance from the sun is about 58 million kilometers. Its diameter is approximately 4879km. It takes 88 Earth days to orbit the sun. It experiences extreme temperature differences of 430Degrees Celcius (Daytime) and -180Degrees Celcius (Nighttime) since it has no atmosohere. It is rocky and full of craters. It has no moons.


Venus
-Is the second planet from the sun and is often called Earth's twin because it is similar in size and structure. It is the hottest planet due to the greenhouse effect with temperature reaching up to 462Degrees Celcius in our system. It is also the brightest planet often seen in the sky from earth visible to the naked eye at night. Its distance from the Sun is about 108million. It takes 243Earth days to rotate unlike most of planets it rotate backwards. It is rocky with volcanoes, mountains and vast plains. It has no moon.




Earth
-Is the third planet from the sun and the only known planet that support life. Its atmosphere is made of 78%Nitrogen, 21%Oxygen, and small amounts of other gases. Its diameter is 12742km. Its distance from the sun is about 149.6million km. Its takes approximately 24hours to rotate on its own axis through an angel of 360Degrees. Its revolution periods are 365.25days or 366days when it is a leap year around the sun. It is an obsolate spheroid. It is 29.2% made of land and 70.8% made of water. It has one natural satellite, which is simply called the Moon.




Mars
-Is the forth planet from the sun and is often called the Red Planet because of its reddish appearance caused by iron oxide on its surface. Its surface is rocky with no liquid. It contains Olympus Mons-the largest volcano in the solar system . It has two small moons which are Phobos and Deimos. Its diameter is approximately 6779km. Its distance from the sun is approximately 227.9million km.




Jupiter
-It is the largest planet in our solar system and the fifth planet from the sun. Its distance from the sun is approximately 778million km. The length of a day in earth's hours is 10hours. It takes 12 earth's years to revolve around the sun. Its diameter is approximately 143000km. Jupiter has a total of 95confirmed moons with the most famous ones known as Galilean Moons
  1. Io - very volcanic.
  2. Europa - icy with a possible ocean.
  3. Ganymede - largest moon in our solar system.
  4. Callisto - heavily cratered.
-It is a gas giant hence has no solid surface.




Saturn
-It is the sixth planet from the sun and the second largest planet in our solar system. It is mostly famous for its beatiful and complex system of rings. It is a gas giant, made mostly of hydrogen and helium, hence has no solid surface. Its diameter is approximately 120536km. Its distance from the sun is approximately 1.43billion km. It has over 140moons and moon-like bodies, the largest moon being Titan-second largest moon in our solar system. It takes approximately 29.5 earth years to revolve round the sun.




Uranus
-It is the seventh planet from the sun. It is an ice giant because it contains icy materials like water, methane, and ammonia. Its appearance is a pale blue-green color caused by methane gas in the upper atmosphere. Its diameter is approximately 50724km. It takes approximately 84earth year to revolve around the sun in one complete rotation. It is the coldest planet in our solar system with average temperature around -224Degrees Celcius. It has 27 known moons which which some are Titania, Oberon, Ariel, Umbriel, and Miranda.




Neptune
-It is the eighth and farthest planet from the sun. Its apperance is rich deep blue color caused by methane gas. It is known for the Great Dark Spot, a storm similar to Jupiter's Great Red Spot. Its diameter is 49244km. It takes approximately 165 Earth years to revolve around the sun. Its distance from the sun is approximately 4.5billion km. It is also an ice giant made of Hydrogen and Helium. It has five faint rings. It has 14 known moons with the largest moon being Triton
